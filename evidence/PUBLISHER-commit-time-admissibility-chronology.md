# Commit-Time Admissibility Gate — Chronology and Disclosure Intake

## Purpose

Collect attributable factual evidence without inferring conception, inventorship, ownership, public-disclosure effect, priority, or filing deadlines.

## Required event records

For each event, record the event type, exact or bounded date, source path or stable identifier, immutable hash or commit where available, author or contributor, audience, access conditions, confidentiality posture, correction or supersession relationship, and reviewer notes.

Seek evidence for the earliest:

- articulation of a binding commit boundary;
- candidate post-state evaluation;
- separation of sufficiency from substantive admissibility;
- fail-closed behavior for missing evidence, authority, observability, or safety conditions;
- recoverability evaluation under lag or disturbance;
- conservative certified-safe subset or recoverable-region formulation;
- reserve, barrier, or distance-to-failure quantity;
- non-compensable hard trigger;
- canonical decision or commit receipt;
- replay, reconstruction, or forensic-review mechanism;
- recovery action following denial or boundary crossing;
- executable implementation, test, benchmark, demonstration, release, or deployment;
- internal disclosure; and
- public disclosure, publication, offer, demonstration, or unrestricted repository access.

## Prohibited inferences

Repository dates, document-internal dates, conversation timestamps, packet dates, or commit dates must not be treated automatically as conception dates, enabling public-disclosure dates, priority dates, or statutory deadline bases.
