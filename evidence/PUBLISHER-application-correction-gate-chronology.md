# Application Correction Gate — Chronology and Disclosure Intake

## Purpose

Collect attributable factual records for the candidate family without inferring conception, inventorship, ownership, public-disclosure consequences, priority, or deadlines.

## Current evidence boundary

The verified adjacent source supports validation refusal, block-and-log behavior, correction and retry for malformed output, owner notification, escalation, and audit records. It does not establish a distinct application-state correction gate, corrected-state object, before-and-after receipt chain, rollback, supersession, or correction authority.

## Required chronology events

For each event, record the exact date or bounded date range, contributor, source location, immutable identifier where available, what was actually described or implemented, intended audience, access conditions, and supporting attachment.

1. Earliest articulation of correcting an application state after validation failure.
2. Earliest distinction between malformed-output correction and application-state correction.
3. Earliest corrected-state representation or object.
4. Earliest comparison of original, rejected, and corrected states.
5. Earliest receipt relationship binding the original and corrected states.
6. Earliest definition of correction authority or who may authorize the correction.
7. Earliest pre-commit admissibility evaluation of a corrected state.
8. Earliest rollback, supersession, replacement, or invalidation behavior.
9. Earliest retry-limit, escalation, quarantine, or terminal-failure behavior.
10. Earliest executable gate, schema, workflow, test, fixture, trace, demonstration, or deployment.
11. Internal disclosures, including recipient, access restrictions, and purpose.
12. Public disclosures, including exact material, publication location, access date, and whether the disclosed material contained the complete technical combination.
13. Corrections, retractions, superseding versions, or abandoned approaches.

Each evidence item should include:

```text

```

## Resumption condition

Automation may populate a limitation-level map and bounded disclosure only after a first-party source establishes at least one direct application-state correction element beyond malformed-output correction. Full combination drafting remains blocked until corrected-state representation, authority, receipt linkage, and failure behavior are supported.

## Legal boundary

This intake does not determine conception, inventorship, ownership, novelty, patentability, disclosure consequences, filing strategy, or any statutory deadline.
