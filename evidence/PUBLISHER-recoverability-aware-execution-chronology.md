# Recoverability-Aware Execution Boundary — Factual Chronology Intake

## Purpose

Record attributable first-party chronology without inferring conception, inventorship, ownership, public-disclosure consequences, priority, or filing deadlines.

## Required event records

For each event, provide:

- event identifier;
- exact date or bounded date range;
- contributor or witness;
- contribution or event description;
- repository, document, message, notebook, diagram, test, demonstration, or recording path;
- immutable commit, hash, message identifier, or other stable reference;
- confidentiality or access status at the time;
- whether the material was shared outside a confidential group;
- relationship to recoverability evaluation, consequence horizons, calibration, safe mode, quarantine, or reconstruction receipts;
- later correction, supersession, or contradiction references.

## Events to locate

1. Earliest articulation of post-execution recoverability as an execution condition.
2. Earliest use of recoverability, observability, absorption capacity, and coherence as a joint threshold set.
3. Earliest distinction among `DENY`, `FAIL_CLOSED`, and post-crossing `QUARANTINE`.
4. Earliest candidate post-state construction or simulation.
5. Earliest calibration or certified-safe-subset work.
6. Earliest executable gate, test, trace, demonstration, or deployment.
7. Earliest internal disclosure to each identified contributor or reviewer.
8. Earliest public post, paper, repository release, talk, demonstration, or other non-confidential disclosure.
