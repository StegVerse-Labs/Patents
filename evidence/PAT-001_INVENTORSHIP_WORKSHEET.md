# PAT-001 Human Inventorship Worksheet

**Patent family:** PAT-001 — Transition-Table-Native Dynamic Micro-Node Computing

**Purpose:** Record human contribution to conception of each claimed limitation. This worksheet is a fact-gathering artifact for qualified legal review. It does not determine inventorship.

## Governing distinctions

For each limitation, record separately:

- conception of the complete operative idea;
- contribution to a material part of that idea;
- implementation or reduction to practice;
- testing or verification;
- drafting, prompting, editing, or review;
- repository ownership or commit authorship.

Implementation, prompting, review, employment, repository ownership, or commit authorship alone must not be treated as inventorship.

## Required evidence fields

Every candidate contribution should identify:

1. contributor's legal name;
2. limitation or combination conceived;
3. earliest known conception date or range;
4. documentary evidence and canonical location;
5. corroborating person or independent record;
6. whether the idea was complete enough for a skilled implementer to practice without further inventive contribution;
7. whether later changes materially altered the claimed concept;
8. uncertainty or conflicting evidence.
