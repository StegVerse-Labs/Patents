## Exact technical blocker

Automation cannot verify the complete family until first-party evidence establishes or expressly defines:

1. a generalized disclosure-object schema;
2. publication-packet construction;
3. secrecy classification and claim-sensitive redaction controls;
4. explicit separation among intake, awareness, validation, closure, publication, activation, custody, execution, and admissibility;
5. a production closure-receipt writer and retained closure receipts;
6. a downstream publication authorization or refusal engine;
7. production custody and reconstruction behavior;
8. retained positive and negative authoritative runtime traces;
9. populated chronology and factual contributor records.
