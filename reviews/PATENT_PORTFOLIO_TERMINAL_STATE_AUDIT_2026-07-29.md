
# Patent Center boundary

No Patent Center screen is currently actionable. Clerical filing begins only after a family has all of the following:

```text
counsel-approved final filing packet
resolved inventorship
resolved ownership or assignment
owner filing authorization
approved drawings and documents
authorized signatures and certifications
fee and entity-status instructions
```

Only then may the authorized human:

1. create or select the application in Patent Center;
2. upload the approved documents;
3. enter verified applicant, inventor, correspondence, entity-status, and priority data;
4. complete authorized signatures and certifications;
5. pay authorized fees;
6. submit;
7. download the official filing receipt;
8. save it under the family filing-receipt path;
9. record the actual application number and filing date;
10. allow automation to calculate deadlines from that actual filing event.
