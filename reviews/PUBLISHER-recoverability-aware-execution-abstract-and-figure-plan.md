# Recoverability-Aware Execution Boundary — Working Abstract and Figure Plan

## Working abstract

A governed execution system evaluates a proposed transition at or before a binding consequence boundary by constructing or identifying a candidate post-execution state and testing recoverability, observability, absorption capacity, and coherence retention over an applicable lag or inference window. Execution is admitted only when required conditions remain within an accepted region or conservative certified approximation. Otherwise, the transition is denied or failed closed before commitment, or quarantined, contained, or escalated after consequence crossing. The system preserves a reconstructable record of the proposal, evaluated state, threshold basis, decision, and resulting containment posture.

This abstract is technical working material derived from verified written-description sources. It is not a filed abstract, final claim construction, or patentability conclusion.
