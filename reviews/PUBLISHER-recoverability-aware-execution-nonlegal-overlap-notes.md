# Recoverability-Aware Execution Boundary — Non-Legal Overlap Notes

## Purpose

These notes organize technical overlap questions for later practitioner review. They are not prior-art conclusions and do not determine novelty, non-obviousness, eligibility, infringement, inventorship, ownership, or legal family scope.

## Commit-Time Admissibility Gate

Shared technical territory:

- evaluation at a binding commit boundary;
- candidate post-state construction;
- fail-closed decision behavior;
- recoverability and consequence evaluation;
- bounded outcomes and decision records.

Potential Recoverability-specific center:

- joint recoverability, observability, absorption-capacity, and coherence thresholds;
- consequence-horizon location and crossing behavior;
- conservative certified-safe execution regions;
- explicit DENY-before, FAIL_CLOSED-at, and QUARANTINE-after treatment;
- lag or inference-window analysis tied to execution consequence.

Open question: whether these elements form an independent family, a dependent embodiment, a continuation candidate, or a claim cluster within Commit-Time Admissibility Gate.

## AI Output-to-Action Boundary

Shared technical territory:

- separation of information output from authority to execute;
- authority-bearing transition before action;
- commit-time refusal and retained evidence.

Potential Recoverability-specific center:

- evaluating physical or computational consequence after a proposed action is authorized but before it becomes binding;
- determining whether execution remains inside recoverable and observable capacity;
- applying containment or quarantine after a consequence horizon is crossed.

Open question: whether Output-to-Action governs authority acquisition while Recoverability-Aware Execution governs consequence admissibility after authority is established.

## Consequence Horizon Formalism

Shared technical territory:

- binding or irreversible consequence boundaries;
- degraded observability or recoverability;
- containment and adaptation after crossing.

Potential patent-family issue:

- CHF is a formal-source layer, while the candidate family would require a concrete computing architecture that applies those conditions to execution decisions, thresholds, traces, and receipts.
