# PAT-003 Technical Comparison Research Ledger

## Controlled family

`PAT-003 — Generalized Adaptive Scanner Using Dynamic Micro-Nodes`

## Purpose

This is a bounded technical-comparison framework. It contains no verified external prior-art result and makes no novelty, obviousness, patentability, validity, infringement, or freedom-to-operate conclusion.

## Working technical center

The current family record concerns a scanner that constructs acquisition, decoding, and comparative micro-nodes from observed signal conditions; allocates computation according to signal strength or ambiguity; evaluates transformations across subsystems; observes until a manifest-relative completeness condition or minimum adverse-condition interval is reached; and returns explicit non-inference when evidence remains insufficient.

## Comparison clusters

### Adaptive sensing and active perception

Questions:

- Does the system alter sensor selection, sampling rate, observation duration, or processing depth based on uncertainty?
- Is adaptation limited to acquisition parameters, or does it construct bounded processing nodes?
- Is insufficient evidence represented explicitly rather than forced into a classification?

### Dynamic computation and microservice construction

Questions:

- Are temporary processing components created according to detected conditions?
- Is their capability bounded to a manifest-defined scope?
- Are construction, exercised capability, and expiry reconstructably recorded?

### Multi-sensor fusion and comparative transformation

Questions:

- Are signals merely fused into a classifier, or compared by how subsystems transform state-bearing inputs?
- Are source, path, receiver, and coupling behavior distinguished?
- Is a multidimensional source-to-return delta retained?

### Sequential testing and stopping rules

Questions:

- Does the system continue observation until a completeness or confidence rule is met?
- Is there a defined minimum adverse-condition interval?
- Does the stopping rule preserve uncertainty and non-inference?

### Anomaly detection and diagnosis

Questions:

- Is the result a generic anomaly score, diagnosis, or bounded observation conclusion?
- Are biological, medical, organism-specific, or diagnostic assertions directly supported?
- Does the system distinguish adaptive fluctuation from dysfunction using verified comparative relationships?

### Event sourcing, receipts, and reconstruction

Questions:

- Are acquisition, node construction, comparisons, decisions, and disposition recorded?
- Can a third party reconstruct why observation continued, stopped, or returned non-inference?
- Are receipts authoritative runtime outputs or conceptual record fields?

### Robotics, industrial inspection, and generalized scanners

Questions:

- Is the scanner modality-specific or generalized by manifest and node construction?
- Does it allocate computation proportionally to signal conditions?
- Does it govern observation and inference through explicit transition boundaries?
