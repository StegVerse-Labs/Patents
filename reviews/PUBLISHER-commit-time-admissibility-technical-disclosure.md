## Technical problem

A proposed transition may be authorized or valid before execution while still producing an inadmissible, unrecoverable, insufficiently observable, or authority-inconsistent state when the transition becomes binding. Pre-action authorization alone does not establish admissibility of the resulting state at commit time.

## Bounded technical sequence

1. Identify the binding or effectively irreversible commit boundary.
2. Bind current state, proposed action, actor or authority basis, destination, evidence, and observability conditions.
3. Construct or identify a candidate post-commit state.
4. Evaluate mandatory evidence and state sufficiency separately from substantive admissibility where supported.
5. Evaluate the candidate post-state rather than relying only on pre-action legitimacy.
6. Evaluate authority, observability, recoverability, coherence, and non-compensable hard-trigger conditions.
7. Optionally evaluate a conservative certified subset and a reserve, barrier margin, or distance-to-failure quantity.
8. Emit a bounded result such as ALLOW, DENY, RESTRICT, DEFER, QUARANTINE, or FAIL_CLOSED.
9. Preserve a canonical decision record for replay, reconstruction, or forensic review.
10. Invoke or identify recovery behavior after denial or an inadmissible crossing where supported.
