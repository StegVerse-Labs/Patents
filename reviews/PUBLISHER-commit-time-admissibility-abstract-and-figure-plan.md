# Commit-Time Admissibility Gate — Working Abstract and Figure Plan

## Working abstract

A system evaluates a proposed transition at a boundary where the transition becomes binding. The system binds a current state, proposed action, authority basis, destination, available evidence, and observability conditions; constructs or identifies a candidate post-transition state; and evaluates mandatory sufficiency and substantive admissibility conditions. The evaluation may include recoverability under lag or disturbance, a conservative certified-safe subset, a reserve or barrier margin, and non-compensable hard-trigger conditions. The system emits a bounded decision and preserves a canonical decision record for reconstruction or recovery. Missing mandatory evidence or unresolved commit-time conditions produce a fail-closed result.

This abstract is a technical drafting aid, not a final patent abstract or legal conclusion.

## Figure plan

1. **Commit-boundary context** — current state, proposed action, actor or authority, destination, evidence, and the binding boundary.
2. **Candidate post-state construction** — transformation from current state and proposed action to the evaluated candidate state.
3. **Two-stage gate** — mandatory sufficiency evaluation followed by substantive admissibility evaluation where supported.
4. **Recoverability region** — robust recoverable region, conservative certified subset, and candidate post-state location.
5. **Reserve and hard-trigger evaluation** — continuous reserve or barrier margin alongside non-compensable critical conditions.
6. **Decision paths** — ALLOW, DENY, RESTRICT, DEFER, QUARANTINE, and FAIL_CLOSED.
7. **Decision record** — bound state, action, authority, evidence, condition results, outcome, and reconstruction references.
8. **Recovery path** — denial or inadmissible-crossing response and retained recovery evidence.
