## U.S. Timing Considerations

USPTO guidance states that a provisional application can establish an early effective filing date for a later nonprovisional application, remains pending for 12 months, and must contain a written description supporting the subject matter for which priority will later be claimed.

USPTO guidance also states that an inventor-originated public disclosure may fall within the one-year U.S. grace period, while the same pre-filing disclosure may preclude patenting in foreign countries.

Accordingly:

- do not calculate a definitive statutory deadline until the earliest qualifying public disclosure is verified;
- use 2026-07-13 only as a known disclosure date, not necessarily the first date;
- obtain registered patent-practitioner review promptly;
- do not claim `patent pending` unless and until an application is actually filed;
- do not treat this repository record as a filing.
