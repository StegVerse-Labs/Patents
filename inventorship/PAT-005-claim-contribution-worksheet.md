## Contributor Interview Questions

1. What exact part of the claimed combination did you conceive?
2. What problem were you trying to solve?
3. What alternatives did you consider or reject?
4. When did you communicate the complete concept to another person or record it?
5. Which files, drawings, messages, or tests corroborate the contribution?
6. Did you merely implement instructions already fully conceived by someone else?
7. Did another contributor materially change the concept before implementation?
8. Which claim limitations would not exist without your conceptual contribution?
