## Filing strategy boundary

The USPTO permits a provisional utility application without formal claims or oath, but requires a sufficiently detailed written description, drawings necessary to understand the invention, inventor identification, cover sheet/ADS, and filing fee. A later nonprovisional generally must be filed within 12 months to claim provisional benefit. Only disclosed and adequately supported subject matter can benefit from the earlier date. Source: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/apply/provisional-application

**Do not block drafting of the broader invention on completion of optional experiments**: a proposed embodiment may be described if technically enabling and accurate, but never misrepresent unbuilt hardware, tested scanner performance, clinical capability, or actual experimental validation. A practitioner must determine adequate support.
